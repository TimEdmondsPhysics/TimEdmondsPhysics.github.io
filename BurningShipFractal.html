Burning Ship Fractal Generator

This script is an visualisation of the burning ship fractal in MATLAB. The burning ship fractal is a type of escape-time fractal that is generated by iterating a complex function. The script allows users to explore the intricate patterns of the burning ship fractal by zooming in on different regions of the complex plane and adjusting the number of escape iterations. The resulting images demostrate the self-similar patterns in the fractal and the beauty of mathematical structures.
